## The Top 3 AI Trends Being Bought on Fiverr

### Trend 1 — AI Coaching / AI Consulting & Training (your category)

Businesses and individuals are paying freelancers to *teach them* how to use AI — not just to do AI work for them. Fiverr's own trend data attributes this to a persistent knowledge gap: most buyers don't understand what tools like AI agents are or how to apply them, and they hire coaches and consultants to close that gap. Fiverr now has dedicated marketplace categories for AI consulting and "AI lessons," and demand for AI-related freelance skills grew roughly **109% year-over-year** across major platforms as of early 2026.

**What buyers search:** "AI consultant," "AI coach," "AI training for my team," "teach me ChatGPT/Claude," "AI adoption strategy," "AI readiness assessment."

### Trend 2 — AI Agents & Workflow Automation (the biggest of the three)

The single most explosive category on Fiverr. Searches for **AI agent expertise surged 18,347%** in Fiverr's Business Trends Index, with related automation-stack demand following: **Go High Level +1,489%**, **Make.com +1,083%**, **n8n AI automation +125%**, **AI voice agents +49%**, plus strong growth in AI-integrated app and website builds (+92% and +39%). Buyers want chatbots, autonomous "digital colleague" agents that handle email/scheduling/customer questions, and end-to-end workflow automations — and they want freelancers who can deliver *measurable business outcomes*, not demos.

**What buyers search:** "AI agent," "AI chatbot for my business," "n8n / Make automation," "AI customer service agent," "AI voice agent," "workflow automation."

### Trend 3 — AI Content & Video Creation (including "humanizing" AI output)

The fastest-growing creative category. **AI video generation/editing grew ~329% year-over-year** (the fastest-growing skill on Upwork), Fiverr's Video & Animation AI demand grew **+278%**, faceless **YouTube automation channels +239%**, and — notably — searches for freelancers who can **"humanize AI content" grew +641%** (rewriting chatbot scripts, marketing emails, and web copy so it doesn't sound machine-generated). Adjacent surges: Substack (+2,028%) and Beehiiv (+1,211%) newsletter builds.

**What buyers search:** "AI video," "UGC video," "faceless YouTube channel," "humanize AI content," "AI ghostwriting," "prompt engineering."

### Gaps and risks

1. **Discoverability.** Buyers search generic terms ("AI consultant," "AI coach for business"), not education jargon. Gigs titled around institutional transformation language will not surface in Fiverr search.
2. **Niche sizing.** "AI coaching for universities" is a narrow Fiverr buyer pool with long sales cycles — Fiverr buyers skew SMB and individual. Your education depth should be the *proof*, not the *gate*: coach any professional/SMB on AI adoption, and let the higher-ed credentials do the trust-building.
3. **Missing productization.** Fiverr rewards fixed-scope packages. Consulting positioned as bespoke engagements needs translation into tiered gigs (see below).
4. **Trend 2 absence.** The 18,347% category is one you can credibly enter at the *strategy/roadmap* level (agent readiness assessments, agent workflow design) even if you don't code the agents yourself — or you subcontract the build.

## Recommended Gig Lineup

**Trend 1 — AI coaching (core, launch first)**
- "I will coach you 1-on-1 on using AI in your business or career" — Basic: 1 session / Standard: 3 sessions + AI-readiness assessment / Premium: monthly coaching + custom AI toolkit.

- "I will humanize your AI-written content" or education-marketing content — lowest strategic fit; skip unless you want volume gigs.
