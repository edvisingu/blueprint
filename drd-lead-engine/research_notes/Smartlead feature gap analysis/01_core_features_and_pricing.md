# Smartlead.ai — Core Product Feature Inventory and Pricing/Packaging

## Email sending infrastructure: mailbox connection methods and per-plan mailbox limits

### Takeaway
Smartlead supports Google OAuth one-click, generic SMTP/IMAP (including Gmail via app password), and Microsoft/Outlook, plus a first-party mailbox resale marketplace (SmartSenders) spanning several third-party vendors. The widely-advertised "unlimited mailboxes" claim is a **marketing/pricing-page claim that is directly contradicted by Smartlead's own mailbox-connection help article**, which says the mailbox limit depends on your plan and must be read from Settings → Billing. This is the single most important unresolved conflict in this research.

### Cited Findings
- Google OAuth: a one-click flow using Smartlead's own pre-verified Google app, with **no Google Cloud project required** on the user's side; Google Workspace accounts typically require Workspace **admin approval** of the app — [Gmail/Google oAuth 1 Click Authentication, helpcenter art. 18](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/18-gmailgoogle-oauth-1-click-authentication)
- Gmail via SMTP/IMAP requires a Google **app password**, which in turn requires 2-Step Verification to be enabled first; documented hosts/ports are **smtp.gmail.com:465** and **imap.gmail.com:993** — [Connect Gmail With SMTP, helpcenter art. 4](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/4-connect-gmail-with-smtp)
- API can add a mailbox programmatically: `POST` to the email-accounts save endpoint with an app password, authenticating via an `api_key` query parameter; **SMTP and IMAP credentials are validated at the moment the account is added** — [Add SMTP Email Account, API reference](https://api.smartlead.ai/reference/create-an-email-account); [Email Accounts, API core docs](https://api.smartlead.ai/core/email-accounts)
- **"Unlimited email accounts at no extra cost on all plans"** appears on Smartlead's pricing page and is repeated on its blog (e.g. the $39/mo Base plan described as including unlimited Gmail connections) — [Smartlead pricing](https://www.smartlead.ai/pricing); [How to Use Smartlead With Gmail](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/how-to-use-smartlead-with-gmail-for-smart-ai-driven-outreach)
- **Contradiction:** the help-centre article on connecting a mailbox states the number of connectable mailboxes **depends on your Smartlead plan** and instructs users to check their limit at **Settings → Billing** — [How to Connect Your Mailbox to Smartlead, helpcenter art. 446](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/446-how-to-connect-your-mailbox-to-smartlead)
- The 14-day free trial explicitly includes **unlimited mailbox connections**, alongside 2,500 email sends and 1,250 contact storage; **warmup emails are counted separately and do not consume the send limit** — [What are the limitations in the free trial?, helpcenter art. 151](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/151-what-are-the-limitations-in-the-free-trial)
- SmartSenders is a multi-vendor mailbox marketplace inside Smartlead. Documented vendors and their per-domain mailbox ratios: **Google and Zapmail/InboxKit — 1–5 mailboxes per domain**; **Outlook via InfraInbox — minimum 2 domains, maximum 20 domains, 3–5 mailboxes per domain**; **Mailreef SMTP — minimum order of 15 domains × 5 mailboxes = 75 mailboxes**; **Smartlead's own pre-warmed mailboxes — up to 3 mailboxes per domain (1:3 ratio)** — [SmartSenders Pricing, art. 266](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/266-smartsenders-pricing); [Microsoft Outlook Mailboxes by Infrainbox, art. 258](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/258-microsoft-outlook-mailboxes-by-infrainbox-setup-pricing-and-deliverability-guide); [SMTP Accounts by Mailreef, art. 259](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/259-smtp-accounts-by-mailreef-faqs); [Pre-warmed Mailboxes by Smartlead, art. 429](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/429-pre-warmed-mailboxes-by-smartlead); [Google and Outlook Mailboxes by InboxKit, art. 419](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/419-google-and-outlook-mailboxes-by-inboxkit-full-admin-access-to-google-and-outlook)
- SmartSenders prices **conflict between Smartlead's own help-centre articles**: Google/Outlook quoted at **$15/domain/year + $4/inbox/month** in one article vs **$13/domain/year + $4.50/mailbox/month (InboxKit)** in another; InfraInbox Outlook quoted at **$16/domain/year + $5/mailbox/month** in one place and **$15/domain/year + $1/inbox/month** in another. The help centre itself says per-vendor limits and prices "are displayed during the setup process itself" — [SmartSenders Pricing, art. 266](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/266-smartsenders-pricing); [SmartSenders FAQs and Troubleshooting, art. 256](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/256-smartsenders-faqs-and-troubleshooting-everything-you-need-to-know)
- SmartSenders Zapmail/InboxKit mailboxes are advertised as giving **full admin access** to the underlying Google/Outlook tenant — [art. 277](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/277-google-and-outlook-mailboxes-by-zapmail-and-it-s-benefits-full-admin-access); [art. 419](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/419-google-and-outlook-mailboxes-by-inboxkit-full-admin-access-to-google-and-outlook)
- Sending-limit guidance conflicts inside Smartlead's own material: the blog cites **2,000 emails/day for Google Workspace**, while the API docs cite **50/day as Google's recommended Gmail limit** — [Gmail & Google Workspace Sending Limits](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/gmail-sending-limits); [Email Accounts, API core docs](https://api.smartlead.ai/core/email-accounts)
- There is a documented set of mailbox **disconnection reasons** surfaced in-app, implying active connection-health monitoring — [Understanding Email Account Disconnection Reasons, art. 65](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/65-understanding-email-account-disconnection-reasons-in-smartlead)

### Inferences
- The most defensible reading of "unlimited mailboxes": **the subscription does not meter the number of connected sending identities, but it does meter emails sent per month and contacts stored.** The monetisation moves from seats/mailboxes to volume (sends) and to the mailbox supply chain (SmartSenders domains/inboxes, billed separately per domain/year + per inbox/month). "Unlimited mailboxes" is therefore true-but-hollow as a cost claim: you still pay per mailbox, just to SmartSenders rather than to the Smartlead plan line item.
- Given art. 446 points to Settings → Billing for a per-plan cap, the likeliest state is that a cap field exists in billing (possibly set very high or to "unlimited" on current plans) and the article is written generically. But this is inference, not confirmed.
- Microsoft 365/Outlook connection is clearly supported (Outlook mailbox vendors are sold through SmartSenders, and Outlook appears throughout), but I did **not** find a help article confirming whether Microsoft connection uses **OAuth/modern auth** or only SMTP/IMAP with app passwords. Do not assume OAuth for Microsoft.

### Gaps
- **Unconfirmed:** the actual numeric mailbox cap per plan, if any. Sources directly conflict (pricing page "unlimited" vs art. 446 "depends on your plan"). I trust art. 446 as more likely to reflect an enforcement mechanism, and the pricing page as more likely to reflect current commercial policy — but this needs an in-app check at Settings → Billing.
- **Unconfirmed:** whether Microsoft 365/Outlook connects via OAuth or only SMTP/IMAP; no primary doc found.
- **Unconfirmed:** current SmartSenders prices. Smartlead's own articles contradict each other and the help centre defers to the live setup flow.

## Inbox/mailbox rotation and sending controls (caps, ramp-up, windows, time zones, jitter)

### Takeaway
Rotation across the connected mailbox pool is real and documented at the level of symptoms and troubleshooting rather than as a documented algorithm. The concrete, documented controls are: per-mailbox daily total limit, per-mailbox minimum time gap, campaign-level sending window / active days / time zone / send interval / new-leads-per-day, and per-mailbox warmup with daily ramp-up. Jitter exists but is described only in marketing terms.

### Cited Findings
- Campaign **Settings → schedule** section documents: **timezone, campaign start date, active days, sending window, send interval, and new leads per day**; the article also states Smartlead "adds small timing variations on its own" — [How To Create an Email Campaign In Smartlead (the new version), art. 442](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/442-how-to-create-an-email-campaign-in-smartlead-the-new-version)
- A mailbox's **daily limit resets at midnight in the account-level time zone**, so a mismatch between account time zone and campaign time zone reduces realised volume — [My campaign is not sending emails or sending less volume, art. 228](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/228-my-campaign-is-not-sending-emails-or-sending-less-volume)
- **A mailbox used in several campaigns splits its daily cap across them** — each campaign does not get the mailbox's full allowance. This is a documented behaviour with direct architectural implications — [art. 228](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/228-my-campaign-is-not-sending-emails-or-sending-less-volume); [Issues with the number of emails being sent out, art. 58](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/58-issues-with-the-number-of-emails-being-sent-out)
- The campaign **"email will be sent every" interval must be larger than the mailbox's minimum time gap**, or sending triggers get skipped; the help centre advises keeping the campaign value **at least 2 minutes above** the mailbox value — [art. 228](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/228-my-campaign-is-not-sending-emails-or-sending-less-volume)
- Campaign **"max new leads per day" excludes follow-ups** — follow-up sends are additional to that number — [art. 228](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/228-my-campaign-is-not-sending-emails-or-sending-less-volume)
- Recommended per-mailbox daily limit is given as **40–50 total including cold emails and warmups** in the help centre, and **30–50 per mailbox per day** on the blog — [art. 58](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/58-issues-with-the-number-of-emails-being-sent-out); [Email Warm-Up Guide](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/email-warm-up-guide)
- Campaign time zone is independently editable — [How to Change the Time Zone of the Email Campaign?, art. 191](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/191-how-to-change-the-time-zone-of-the-email-campaign)
- API uses **IANA time zone names** (e.g. `America/New_York`) — [Full API Documentation, art. 125](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/125-full-api-documentation)
- Warmup API/field names: **`warmup_enabled`, `total_warmup_per_day`, `daily_rampup`, `reply_rate_percentage`** on account creation and warmup-settings update — [Warmup Settings, API reference](https://api.smartlead.ai/api-reference/email-accounts/warmup-settings.md); [Email Warmup guide, API](https://api.smartlead.ai/guides/email-warmup)
- Warmup can be **bulk-updated across mailboxes from the Email Accounts tab**; guidance is to keep warmup at roughly **20–30% of sending volume** — [How to bulk update warmup for mailboxes, art. 269](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/269-how-to-bulk-update-warmup-for-mailboxes)
- Per-account warmup config documents a **daily warmup volume** setting (suggested 10–20 for mailboxes also running active campaigns) and a **ramp-up option for fresh domains** — [How to enable AI email account warm ups, art. 52](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/52-how-to-enable-ai-email-account-warm-ups)
- There is an **auto-adjust warmup/sending ratio** ("smart adjusting algorithm") that operates **at the individual mailbox level, not the campaign level**, and reduces warmup volume while campaigns are active — [Smartlead's smart adjusting algorithm for mailbox warmups, art. 63](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/63-smartlead-s-smart-adjusting-algorithm-for-mailbox-warmups)
- **Weekday-only warmup** is an available toggle — [How to enable weekday only warm up emails, art. 275](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/275-how-to-enable-weekday-only-warm-up-emails-in-smartlead)
- There is a **warmup pool** concept with tiering: the Unlimited Smart tier adds a **Premium warm-up pool**, and pool/upgrade eligibility has its own article — [Understand Your Warmup Pool and Upgrade Eligibility, art. 438](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/438-understand-your-warmup-pool-and-upgrade-eligibility); [Smartlead Pricing Plans, art. 439](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/439-smartlead-pricing-plans)
- **Marketing-only (flagged):** the claim that Smartlead "adjusts the exact send time within your chosen window and adds delays between each email, ranging from seconds to minutes" and "rotates through connected inboxes in real time", "distributes them across your connected inbox pool, keeping each individual inbox within safe daily limits" — these appear in blog copy, not product documentation, and no algorithm, distribution policy or jitter range is documented — [Email Warm-Up Guide](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/email-warm-up-guide); [Cold Email Checklist](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/cold-email-checklist)
- **Delivery optimization** is a distinct settings tab covering **plain-text mode, "enhanced sending", and turning open/click tracking off** — [art. 442](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/442-how-to-create-an-email-campaign-in-smartlead-the-new-version)

### Inferences
- The combination of "mailbox daily cap is split across campaigns" + "mailbox daily limit resets on the *account* time zone while campaigns have their *own* time zone" + "campaign interval must exceed mailbox minimum gap or triggers are silently skipped" means **Smartlead's scheduler is a per-mailbox budget allocator with campaign-level pacing layered on top, and the two layers can fight each other.** This is a well-documented source of under-sending and is the clearest design lesson for anyone building a competing engine: make the budget authority single and make skipped triggers observable.
- Warmup appears to be a genuinely separate send stream from campaign sends (it does not consume the plan's monthly send allowance per art. 151), and it is reconciled against campaign volume by a per-mailbox auto-adjust (art. 63).
- No documentation found for a **per-mailbox ramp-up schedule for campaign sends** (as opposed to warmup sends). `daily_rampup` is documented as a warmup field. Do not assume campaign-send ramp-up exists.

### Gaps
- **Unconfirmed:** the rotation distribution policy (round-robin vs weighted by remaining budget vs health score), and whether rotation is per-lead, per-step or per-thread. No Smartlead doc found that specifies it. Only marketing-level description exists.
- **Unconfirmed:** the actual jitter/random-delay range, and whether it is user-configurable. Marketing says "seconds to minutes"; no documented setting found.
- **Unconfirmed:** whether follow-up steps are sent from the **same mailbox** as the original step (thread affinity). Not addressed in any source found, and it materially affects threading.
- **Unconfirmed:** whether campaign-send (not warmup) ramp-up schedules exist as a first-class control.

## Master Inbox / Unibox: what it is, what you can do, documented limits

### Takeaway
Master Inbox (currently marketed as "Master Inbox" with a prior "Unified Master Inbox 3.0" generation) is a unified reply view across all mailboxes and channels, with AI reply categorisation, per-lead and bulk actions, assignment to teammates, and keyboard shortcuts. **No published quantitative limits on Master Inbox actions were found** — the only numeric constraint surfaced is a filter-selection cap of 3 subcategories per category, and even the category count is inconsistent across Smartlead's own pages.

### Cited Findings
- Master Inbox consolidates replies from **every channel and every mailbox into one view**, with automatic reply categorisation — [Unified Inbox for Cold Email Replies](https://www.smartlead.ai/unified-inbox); [Smartlead Master Inbox in 2026: Reply Categorization, Multi-Channel, Agency Dashboard, and Bulk Actions](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/master-inbox-new-features)
- Reply categories cited include **interested, not interested, out of office, wrong person** — but the **count conflicts across Smartlead's own pages: one lists six categories, another lists seven subcategories plus a "Team member" option** — [Master Inbox new features blog](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/master-inbox-new-features); [Inbox Management When Sending Bulk Emails](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/inbox-management-with-smartlead-when-sending-bulk-emails)
- **Bulk actions** listed on the Master Inbox update blog: **bulk categorize, bulk assign, bulk move to campaign, bulk block, bulk export**. The unified-inbox product page lists a partly different set: **bulk tag, assign, archive, reply, forward** — [Master Inbox new features blog](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/master-inbox-new-features); [Unified Inbox page](https://www.smartlead.ai/unified-inbox)
- **Assignment:** a reply can be assigned to a teammate in one click with a note attached, and it stays with that person until resolved; the Master Inbox 3.0 article describes a per-lead **"Assign to" button in the top right** — [Unified Master Inbox 3.0](https://www.smartlead.ai/unified-master-inbox-old); [Unified Inbox page](https://www.smartlead.ai/unified-inbox)
- **Filter limit:** you can select **up to 3 subcategories within each category** in the inbox filter. This is the only explicit numeric limit found, and it governs filtering, not actions — [Master Inbox new features blog](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/master-inbox-new-features)
- **Keyboard shortcuts** are documented for the inbox — [Keyboard shortcuts, art. 170](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/170-keyboard-shortcuts)
- **Archived leads do not count toward active lead limits** — [All Leads: Unlimited Lead Storage, art. 425](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/425-all-leads-unlimited-lead-storage)
- Leads can be **pushed manually from the Master Inbox into a subsequence**, but **leads cannot be moved between subsequences belonging to different parent campaigns** — [Can I push leads from one subsequence to another?, art. 165](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/165-can-i-push-leads-from-one-subsequence-to-another)
- An **agency dashboard** view is part of the same 2026 Master Inbox update — [Master Inbox new features blog](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/master-inbox-new-features)

### Inferences
- Reply, forward, categorise and assign are all available from the Master Inbox; the discrepancy between the two bulk-action lists most likely reflects **product drift between a newer blog post and an older product page**, and the newer blog (bulk categorize/assign/move-to-campaign/block/export) is more likely current. Trust the blog over the `unified-master-inbox-old` page, whose URL slug itself signals it is stale.
- The documented bulk actions are notably **agency-shaped** (move to campaign, block, export, agency dashboard), consistent with Smartlead's higher tiers selling client workspaces and whitelabel.

### Gaps
- **Unconfirmed:** any rate limit, pagination limit, selection-size cap or retention window on Master Inbox. No help-centre article on bulk assign was found at all; the bulk-action descriptions come only from marketing pages.
- **Unconfirmed:** the authoritative list and exact names of reply categories/subcategories. Smartlead's own pages disagree on the count.
- **Unconfirmed:** whether Master Inbox search covers full message bodies, and whether it spans archived leads.

## Sequences: step types, delays, branching, sub-sequences, stop conditions

### Takeaway
The sequence model is a **linear step list, not a visual branching canvas**. Native step types are **Email, Manual Task, and Call (SmartDialer users only)**. Branching is achieved out-of-band via **subsequences** — child campaigns triggered by reply category or reply text — rather than by in-sequence conditional nodes. Stop-on-reply is first-class and configurable (Replies / Clicks / Opens).

### Cited Findings
- Documented sequence **step types: Email, Manual Task, and Call** (Call available to SmartDialer users). **Manual Task pauses the sequence until someone marks the task complete.** Call steps carry a **wait time, an optional calling script, and SDR assignment** — [art. 442](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/442-how-to-create-an-email-campaign-in-smartlead-the-new-version)
- **"When to stop a lead's sequence"** is a campaign-behaviour setting with options **Replies (recommended), Clicks, or Opens** — [art. 442](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/442-how-to-create-an-email-campaign-in-smartlead-the-new-version); [How to do A/B testing with Smartlead?, art. 146](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/146-how-to-do-ab-testing-with-smartlead)
- **A/B variants** can be added per email step for **subject lines or message copies**. Distribution options: **Pattern-based (recommended) or randomized**; the distribution mode **cannot be changed once the campaign is live**. The config exposes **"Manual Distribution"** and **"Split the variant percentage equally"**, plus an **AI option that picks a winner and sends it to the remaining percentage of recipients** — [art. 146](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/146-how-to-do-ab-testing-with-smartlead)
- **Subsequences** are the branching mechanism: the API's create-subsequence endpoint creates a **child campaign with trigger conditions such as reply category or reply text**; **passing an empty conditions array creates a subsequence with no conditions** — [Create Subsequence Campaign, API reference](https://api.smartlead.ai/api-reference/campaigns/create-subsequence); [How to Create Subsequence's: A Guide with Tips and Tricks, art. 215](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/215-how-to-create-subsequence-s-a-guide-with-tips-and-tricks)
- Branch trigger: **AI reply categorisation analyses reply context and conditions can be set on that analysis to route the lead into the appropriate subsequence** — [Create Subsequence Campaign, API reference](https://api.smartlead.ai/api-reference/campaigns/create-subsequence)
- **Hard constraint:** leads **cannot be moved between subsequences belonging to different parent campaigns** — [art. 165](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/165-can-i-push-leads-from-one-subsequence-to-another)
- Sequences are readable/writable via API — [Email Sequences, API core](https://api.smartlead.ai/core/sequences); [Get Campaign Sequences, API reference](https://api.smartlead.ai/api-reference/campaigns/get-sequences)
- **Liquid syntax** is supported inside email templates for conditional show/hide of content based on available lead data, and for **fallbacks** on missing fields; help-centre tips include using single quotes around comparison operators and closing every conditional block — [How to use liquid syntax to win leads / Fallbacks, art. 26](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/26-how-to-use-liquid-syntax-to-win-leads-fallbacks)
- Smartlead's own guidance (advice, not a limit): sequences of about **5–7 steps over 14–21 days**, with **24–48 hours between touches** — [How to Create Multi-channel Outreach Sequences That Convert](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/how-to-create-multi-channel-outreach-sequences)
- **Marketing claim (flagged):** that a reply on LinkedIn automatically stops the email sequence — stated on the blog, not in documentation — [Best LinkedIn Outreach Tools in 2026](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/best-linkedin-outreach-tools)

### Inferences
- "Follow-up on no reply" is implicit rather than an explicit branch: the sequence runs its steps on a delay and the **stop-on-reply condition removes the lead**, which produces no-reply-follow-up behaviour without a conditional node. There is **no evidence of an if/else node inside the sequence editor.**
- The three layers of conditionality are deliberately separated and do not compose in one place: **spintax** (random variation), **Liquid** (per-lead content conditionals within a message), **subsequences** (post-reply routing across campaigns). A competing product with a unified branching canvas would be a genuine differentiator — no Smartlead source documents a single canvas combining them.
- Since subsequences are implemented as **child campaigns**, they likely inherit campaign-level constructs (their own schedule, own mailbox selection, own stop conditions) and the cross-parent movement restriction follows naturally from that modelling choice.

### Gaps
- **Unconfirmed:** the full enumerated list of subsequence trigger conditions (only "reply category" and "reply text" were surfaced), and whether non-reply triggers (opened-not-clicked, bounced, unsubscribed, time-elapsed) can trigger a subsequence.
- **Unconfirmed:** whether there is a maximum number of steps per sequence, maximum A/B variants per step, or maximum subsequences per campaign.
- **Unconfirmed:** whether delays are expressible in hours/minutes or days only, and whether per-step sending-window overrides exist.
- **Unconfirmed:** whether stop-on-reply is cross-channel in all configurations (marketing says LinkedIn replies stop email; no documentation corroborates).

## Spintax: syntax, placement, nesting

### Takeaway
Spintax uses standard brace-pipe syntax, works in both subject lines and body, and supports nesting. Smartlead explicitly states there is **no technical nesting limit**, only practical guidance (3–7 spintax groups per email, 2–4 options each).

### Cited Findings
- Spintax is supported in **both subject lines and body content**; example given: `{Quick question|Curious about something|Idea for} {company}` — [Spintax guide for cold email outreach (2026)](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/what-is-spintax)
- **Nesting** is described as alternatives inside other alternatives, with a two-level worked example; Smartlead states **"there's no technical limit, but there's a practical one"** and recommends **3–7 spintax groups per email with 2–4 options each** — [Spintax guide](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/what-is-spintax)
- Dedicated help-centre article exists for spintax usage — [How do I use spintax in Smartlead?, art. 31](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/31-how-do-i-use-spintax-in-smartlead)
- Smartlead has a help article weighing **spintax vs. multiple A/B variants** as alternative approaches to variation — [Would it be better to use a bunch of variants rather than spintax?, art. 117](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/117-would-it-be-better-to-use-a-bunch-of-variants-rather-than-spintax)
- **Each follow-up step can carry its own spintax**, and spintax is processed alongside custom variables such as `first_name` and `company` — [Spintax guide](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/what-is-spintax)
- **Documentation inconsistency:** Smartlead's own sources write variables **sometimes with single braces and sometimes with double braces**, so the authoritative placeholder delimiter is not reliably documented — observed across [Spintax guide](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/what-is-spintax) and [Liquid syntax art. 26](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/26-how-to-use-liquid-syntax-to-win-leads-fallbacks)

### Inferences
- The single-vs-double brace inconsistency is likely because **spintax uses single braces `{a|b}` while variables/Liquid use double braces `{{field}}`** — but no Smartlead source states this cleanly, so it should be verified in the editor rather than assumed.

### Gaps
- **Unconfirmed:** maximum nesting depth (Smartlead says none; untested), and whether spintax is resolved per-recipient or per-send-batch (affects whether two recipients can receive identical variants).
- **Unconfirmed:** whether spintax works in sender name, preheader, or Manual Task/Call script fields.

## AI features: branding, capability, configurability, model disclosure, cost

### Takeaway
There are three distinct AI surfaces: **SmartAssistant** (in-app chat that writes whole sequences with placeholders), **Reply Agent** (drafts replies inside Master Inbox, human-review-before-send), and **AI reply categorisation** (classifies inbound replies, and drives subsequence routing). **No model is disclosed anywhere I could find.** Credits: SmartAssistant itself is stated to cost nothing on top of the plan, but it consumes the underlying feature's credits (e.g. SmartProspect credits when unlocking emails). There is **no "AI Sensei"** in any Smartlead source.

### Cited Findings

## Lead management: import, custom fields, deduplication, suppression, statuses

### Takeaway
This is the best-documented area, with exact field names and numeric limits in the API docs. Key numbers: **400 leads per API request**, **max 200 custom fields per request**, **40 mapped custom variables per lead in the CSV import UI**, case-sensitive custom-field keys, and a `skipped_leads` array with per-lead skip reasons. Suppression operates as two separate systems: the account's own global block list and a platform-wide community bounce list.

### Cited Findings
- **Batch size: up to 400 leads per request** — [Add Leads to Campaign, API reference](https://api.smartlead.ai/api-reference/campaigns/add-leads); [Lead Management Guide, API](https://api.smartlead.ai/guides/lead-management)
- **Custom fields** are passed in a **`custom_fields` object per lead**; **max 200 fields per request**; **placeholder names in email copy must match key names exactly — keys are case-sensitive** — [Lead Management Guide, API](https://api.smartlead.ai/guides/lead-management)
- **CSV import UI caps mapped custom variables at 40 per lead** — [How to Map CSV Columns in Smartlead Campaigns, art. 409](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/409-how-to-map-csv-columns-in-smartlead-campaigns)
- **Deduplication:** duplicate emails **within the same campaign are always skipped**; **cross-campaign duplicate handling is controlled by the `ignore_duplicate_leads_in_other_campaign` setting** — [Lead Management Guide, API](https://api.smartlead.ai/guides/lead-management); [How do I remove duplicates from different campaigns?, art. 16](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/16-how-do-i-remove-duplicates-from-different-campaigns)
- **Global block list:** blocked addresses are skipped on import and the block **applies across all campaigns in the account**; the API setting **`ignore_global_block_list` defaults to `false`**, and the guide advises leaving it that way — [Lead Management Guide, API](https://api.smartlead.ai/guides/lead-management)
- **Import observability:** the import response includes a **`skipped_leads` array with a per-lead reason**, e.g. **"On global block list"**, **"Already exists in this campaign"** — [Add Leads to Campaign, API reference](https://api.smartlead.ai/api-reference/campaigns/add-leads)
- **Two separate suppression systems:** the account's own global block list, and a **platform-level community bounce list** — which is why a lead can pass your own list and still be blocked on import — [Why Emails Get Blocked on Upload in Cold Email Tools](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/why-emails-get-blocked-on-cold-email-tools)
- **UI wording conflict:** the CSV import UI presents "Import leads even if they are in the Global Block List" as a choice among options and says the bounce option is *recommended* to stay on, while a Smartlead blog says the bounce option is *enabled by default* — [art. 409](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/409-how-to-map-csv-columns-in-smartlead-campaigns); [Why Emails Get Blocked on Upload](https://www.smartlead.ai/blog/why-emails-get-blocked-on-cold-email-tools)
- **Lead lists** are a first-class object with their own import endpoint, separate from campaign-level import — [Import Leads to List, API reference](https://api.smartlead.ai/api-reference/lead-lists/import-leads)
- **Lead categories** exist as an API object group with **get / create / update / delete** operations — [Lead Management Guide, API](https://api.smartlead.ai/guides/lead-management)
- **Archived leads do not count toward active lead limits**; there is a dedicated "All Leads" unlimited lead storage concept — [All Leads: Unlimited Lead Storage, art. 425](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/425-all-leads-unlimited-lead-storage)
- API authentication is via an **`api_key` query parameter** — [Add SMTP Email Account, API reference](https://api.smartlead.ai/reference/create-an-email-account); [Getting Started Guide, API](https://api.smartlead.ai/guides/getting-started)
- Native CRM integrations documented include **HubSpot** (two separate guides, one legacy and one SmartAgents-era) and **HeyReach** — [HubSpot art. 127](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/127-how-to-set-up-hubspot-integration-with-smartlead); [HubSpot SmartAgents art. 445](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/445-smartlead-x-hubspot-integration-guide-smartagents); [HeyReach art. 432](https://helpcenter.smartlead.ai/en/articles/432-smartlead-x-heyreach-integration-guide)

### Inferences
- The **40 (UI) vs 200 (API) custom-field ceiling** is a real asymmetry: the API is substantially more permissive than the CSV mapper. Anyone comparing feature parity should treat 40 as the practical self-serve limit and 200 as the integration limit.
- `api_key` passed as a **query parameter** rather than a header is a dated and leak-prone auth design (query strings land in logs, proxies and referrers). Worth noting as a security-posture differentiator.
- The existence of a platform-wide community bounce list alongside the per-account block list means **suppression is partly outside the customer's control** — a lead can be unreachable for reasons the customer cannot inspect or override except by toggling off the recommended protection.

### Gaps
- **Unconfirmed:** the enumerated set of lead status values (e.g. the exact status/state machine a lead moves through). The API has lead-category CRUD but I found no document enumerating lead statuses.
- **Unconfirmed:** how lead categories interact with the block list and duplicate checks — explicitly not covered in any source found.
